About MockBase

Structured practice systems for high stakes preparation.

MockBase is a lightweight hub for focused practice tools that help users prepare for interviews, viva examinations, academic assessments, grant interviews, language speaking tasks, and other structured assessment settings.

What MockBase is built for

Many candidates do not fail because they know nothing. They fail because their answers are vague, unstructured, unsupported by evidence, or poorly matched to the assessment format. MockBase turns preparation into repeated, structured practice.

For example, a faculty interview candidate can rehearse research vision, teaching philosophy, funding strategy, and department fit. A PhD viva candidate can rehearse contribution, methodology, limitations, originality, and examiner challenges.

Current product areas

Faculty Interview Simulator. Practice academic job interview prompts and prepare structured answers.
PhD Viva Practice Simulator. Practise thesis defence questions and examiner style follow ups.
Behavioural Interview Simulator. Practise evidence based answers using structured examples.
Grant Interview Practice Simulator. Practise feasibility, contribution, impact, team, risk, and project defence questions.

Design principles

  • Structure before fluency. A fluent answer is not enough if the logic is weak.
  • Evidence before claims. Strong answers show what the candidate did, what changed, and why it matters.
  • Practice before performance. Candidates need repeated rehearsal before the real panel, examiner, or committee.
  • Independence before confusion. MockBase is independent and does not claim official affiliation with examination bodies, universities, employers, or government bodies.

Who MockBase helps

MockBase is designed for doctoral candidates, academic job candidates, grant applicants, public sector applicants, international students, language test candidates, and professionals preparing for structured interviews.

How to start

Choose the simulator that matches your current assessment, open the free practice version, and use the guides to understand what strong answers need to demonstrate.

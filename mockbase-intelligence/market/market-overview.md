## Decision rule

Analyse every opportunity as:

`Search Intent × User Pain × Existing Supply × Competitive Density × Purchase Intent × Differentiation`

The demand unit is:

`specific user + use context + desired presentation outcome`

For example: a freelance UI designer preparing a Behance SaaS case study needs a realistic dark-mode onboarding mockup without a slow Photoshop smart-object workflow.

## Classification

- A — High demand / Low supply
- B — High demand / High competition
- C — Low demand / Low competition
- D — Existing supply already adequate
- E — Hidden workflow gap
- F — Insufficient evidence

Default priority: `A > E > B`. Do not build C or D by default. Validate F using the lowest-cost reversible experiment.
